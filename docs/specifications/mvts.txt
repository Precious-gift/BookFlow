BookFlowMinimum Viable Technical Specification (MVTS) v1Requirements Engineering and MVP ScopeVersion: 1.1 — Scope Amendment: Business Profile Management + Platform AdminProject: BookFlow — Appointment Booking + CRM for Small Service BusinessesMVP boundary: Core booking, availability, appointment management, customer CRM, business profile/account management, basic business dashboard, and a limited platform admin portal for business oversight and basic platform analytics.1. Project OverviewBookFlow is a web-based appointment booking and customer relationship management platform for small service businesses. Business owners use BookFlow to configure their business profile, services and availability, manage appointments, maintain customer records, and view basic operational metrics. Customers can book an appointment through a public booking page without creating a BookFlow account. Platform administrators use a separate admin portal to oversee registered businesses and view basic platform-level usage metrics.2. Primary ActorsActorRole in MVPBusiness OwnerCreates and manages the business account, business profile, services, availability, appointments, and customer records.CustomerUses the public booking page to select a service, date and available time, then submits contact information and creates an appointment without a BookFlow account.Platform AdministratorUses a separate authenticated admin portal to oversee registered businesses, view business profiles and basic platform-level usage metrics, and perform limited platform account management such as business activation/deactivation.3. Acceptance CriteriaRequirementAcceptance criteriaFR-01Given valid registration information, when the business owner submits registration, then a BookFlow account is created. Given invalid or missing required information, registration is rejected and the relevant error is shown.FR-02Given a registered business owner provides valid credentials, when the login form is submitted, then the system authenticates the owner and displays the dashboard. Given invalid credentials, access is denied and an appropriate error is shown.FR-03Given an authenticated business owner, when logout is selected, then the authenticated session ends and protected dashboard access is no longer available.FR-04Given valid business profile information, when the owner saves changes, then the updated profile information is displayed on the business account.FR-05Given valid service information, when the owner submits the form, then the service is saved and displayed in the service list. Given required information is missing or invalid, submission is rejected and the invalid fields are identified.FR-06Given an existing service, when the owner changes valid service information and saves, then the updated values are displayed.FR-07Given an active service, when the owner deactivates it, then the service is marked inactive and is no longer offered for new customer bookings.FR-08Given an inactive service, when a customer opens the booking page, then that service cannot be selected for a new booking.FR-09Given working-day and hour values, when the owner saves them, then the configured schedule is used when generating future availability.FR-10Given an unavailable period, when availability is generated for an affected date, then time slots overlapping the unavailable period are not offered.FR-11Given working hours of 09:00–17:00 and a 60-minute service, when a customer selects a date, then only slots that allow the full service duration within available hours are displayed. Existing appointments and unavailable periods are excluded.FR-12Given an active business, when its public booking page is accessed, then customers can access the business's booking interface without accessing the owner dashboard.FR-13Given active services exist, when a customer opens the booking page, then the active services are displayed. Given no active services exist, an appropriate empty state is shown.FR-14Given an active service and available times, when a customer selects the service and date, then available slots satisfying the service duration and availability rules are displayed. Given no slots are available, a clear no-availability message is shown.FR-15Given a customer who does not have a BookFlow account, when valid booking information is submitted, then the customer can create an appointment without registration.FR-16Given a booking form, when the customer submits it without a name or phone number, then the booking is rejected and the required fields are identified. A valid optional email may be supplied.FR-17Given a slot has already been booked, when another customer attempts to book that slot, then the system rejects the booking and indicates that the time is no longer available.FR-18Given valid customer information and a currently available slot, when the customer submits the booking, then an appointment is created with the selected service, date and time.FR-19Given a successful appointment creation, when booking processing finishes, then the customer sees an in-app confirmation containing the relevant booking details. This requirement does not include email, SMS, or WhatsApp notifications.FR-20Given existing appointments, when the owner opens appointment management, then appointments relevant to the business are listed. Given no appointments exist, an appropriate empty state is shown.FR-21Given an appointment exists, when the owner opens it, then the appointment details include the relevant customer, service, date/time, and status information.FR-22Given an appointment exists, when the owner changes its status, then the appointment reflects the selected status. MVP statuses are Pending, Confirmed, Completed, and Cancelled.FR-23Given an existing appointment and an available alternative slot, when the owner selects the new slot, then the appointment date/time is updated. Given no suitable slot exists, rescheduling is prevented and an appropriate message is shown.FR-24Given an existing appointment, when the owner cancels it, then its status changes to Cancelled and its time becomes available for future booking.FR-25Given a customer record already exists for the same business and phone number, when that customer makes another booking, then the booking is associated with the existing customer record rather than creating a duplicate.FR-26Given customer records exist, when the owner opens CRM, then the records are displayed. Given no customer records exist, an appropriate empty state is shown.FR-27Given an existing customer record, when the owner updates valid customer information and saves, then the updated information is displayed.FR-28Given an existing customer record, when the owner adds a text note and saves, then the note is stored and displayed on the customer record.FR-29Given a customer has previous appointments, when the owner opens the customer profile, then previous appointments are displayed with dates, services, and statuses. Given no history exists, an appropriate empty state is shown.FR-30Given the owner dashboard is opened, when dashboard data is available, then it displays today's appointments, upcoming appointments, total customers, and completed appointments.FR-31Given an authenticated business owner, when valid business profile changes are saved, then the updated business name, image, contact information, address, category, currency, bio, and booking-page identity are displayed. Invalid or missing required values are rejected with clear errors.FR-32Given an authenticated business owner, when valid permitted account information or a valid password change is submitted, then the account information is updated and the new credentials/security state are applied. Invalid current-password or password rules prevent the change and show an error.FR-33Given an authorized admin account, when valid admin credentials are submitted, then the admin portal is displayed. Given a non-admin account attempts to access an admin-only resource, access is denied.FR-34Given platform data is available, when the admin dashboard is opened, then it displays total registered businesses, active businesses, total customers, and total appointments. Appropriate loading, empty, and error states are shown where applicable.FR-35Given registered businesses exist, when the admin opens business management, then businesses can be searched/filtered and their basic status/registration information is displayed. Given no matching businesses exist, an appropriate empty state is shown.FR-36Given a registered business exists, when the admin opens its details, then the business profile, owner information, customer count, appointment count, and relevant service/usage information are displayed. Unauthorized users cannot access the details.FR-37Given an authorized admin and an existing business, when the admin changes its permitted account status, then the business status is updated and the resulting state is shown. The action requires authorization.FR-38Given platform data is available, when the admin opens platform analytics, then basic aggregate business/customer/appointment usage information is displayed. No advanced reporting or predictive analytics are required.6.
